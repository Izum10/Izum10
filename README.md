## About

I finished my **Honours Specialization in Computer Science** at Western University. My undergraduate thesis built an automated pipeline for **scoping reviews** in health research, and before that I spent **16 months at Celestica** as a Robotic Process Automation Developer, working on enterprise automation across SAP and Blue Prism.

The thread through most of my work is automating processes people assume have to stay manual, then checking whether the automated version actually holds up against the manual baseline.

Currently working with **Python, Java, React, and Node.js**, and moving further into applied AI.

## Research and Projects

### Undergraduate Thesis: Automated Scoping Review Pipeline
**Western University** · *Supervised by Dr. Pavlos Bobos*

Scoping reviews in health research involve a lot of manual searching, screening, and data extraction. I built a modular pipeline in **Python** using **NLP techniques** and the **PubMed API** to handle those stages as one integrated workflow rather than a set of disconnected tools, then evaluated it on a live physiotherapy review question against a manual baseline.
